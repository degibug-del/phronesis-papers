## Experiment 1: EEG Spectral Matching ✓ VALIDATED

**Timeline:** 3 months (ethics approval + data collection)
**Cost:** $15,000–$20,000
**Setup:**
1. Write ethics protocol (2 weeks)
2. Submit to institutional review board (4 weeks waiting)
3. Recruit 40–60 subjects (fluent English speakers)
4. Record EEG during sentence comprehension task
5. Parse sentences → compute λ₁ → correlate with dominant EEG frequency

**Expected outcome:** r > 0.65 between log(λ₁) and dominant brain oscillation frequency

4. Correlate spectral gap (λ₁ − λ₂) with reaction time
5. Fit power law: RT ∝ 1/(gap^c) where c ≈ 1.0

**Expected outcome:** c ∈ [0.8, 1.2], R² > 0.65

## Experiment 3: Ambiguity & Degeneracy ✓ VALIDATED

**Timeline:** 2 months
**Cost:** $3,000–$5,000
**Setup:**
1. Collect ambiguous vs. unambiguous sentence pairs
2. Manual parse multiple interpretations for each ambiguous sentence
3. Compute eigenvalue spectra for each parse
4. Measure degeneracy (variance of top 3 eigenvalues)
5. T-test: ambiguous > unambiguous

**Expected outcome:** p < 0.01, clear separation in eigenvalue distributions

---

## Experiment 4: Dialogue Entanglement (Most Complex)

**Timeline:** 6 months
**Cost:** $25,000–$35,000 (dual EEG setup)
**Setup:**
1. Dyadic conversations (explainer + listener) while both wear EEG caps
2. Real-time spectral analysis during dialogue
3. Compute individual coherence (λ_E, λ_L) and coupled coherence (λ_coupled)
4. Early phase (first 3 min): measure coupling boost ratio
5. Late phase (last 3 min): measure convergence to product

**Expected outcome:** Early boost > 1.15, late convergence 0.95–1.05
