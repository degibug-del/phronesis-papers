### 1. Identify Your IRB Office
- Go to: [Your University] → Research → Institutional Review Board
- Download their submission checklist
- Get email: irb@[your-institution].edu

### 2. Determine Your IRB Type
- **Expedited Review:** Likely for this study (minimal risk, standard EEG)
- **Full Board Review:** Only if your IRB deems it higher-risk

## SECTION E: SUBMISSION WORKFLOW

### Step 1: Prepare Documents (1 week)
- [ ] Download your institution's IRB forms
- [ ] Customize exp1_irb_protocol.md to your institution's template
- [ ] Write Informed Consent Form (1 page, plain language)
- [ ] Create recruitment flyer
- [ ] Compile researcher CV

### Step 2: Internal Review (1 week)
- [ ] Send draft protocol to your department chair
  - Email: "I'm submitting an IRB protocol for a cognitive neuroscience study. Can you review?"
  - Ask for feedback (1 week turnaround)
- [ ] Send Informed Consent Form to IRB office
  - Email: "Does this ICF meet your institution's requirements?"
  - Ask for template/guidance

### Step 3: Submit to IRB (Timeline: 2–4 weeks for approval)
- [ ] Upload all documents to your IRB's electronic system (usually eIRB or similar)
- [ ] Send cover letter to IRB chair (email)
- [ ] Confirm receipt with IRB office
- [ ] **Expected approval time:** 2–4 weeks (expedited review)

### Step 4: Post-Approval (1 week)
- [ ] Download IRB approval letter
- [ ] Post approval notice in lab
- [ ] Begin subject recruitment

## SECTION G: GO/NO-GO CHECKLIST (August 1)

Proceed to Phase 1 if:

- [ ] **Theory paper finalized** (spectral gap integrated, v2 ready)
- [ ] **NSF grant submitted to grants office** (internal review passed)
- [ ] **IRB protocol submitted to institutional IRB** (expecting approval by Aug 15)
- [ ] **EEG lab access confirmed** (50 subjects, Oct–Dec capacity)
- [ ] **First graduate RA identified** (hired or committed)

**If all ✅:** Full speed ahead. Begin recruitment immediately upon IRB approval.

**If any 🔴:** Delay Phase 1 start. Focus on missing component. Phase 2 can start in parallel.

- [ ] Protocol title is clear and specific
