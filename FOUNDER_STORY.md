# Diego Rincón: Founder Story

## The Origin

I grew up trying to understand how minds work.

In high school, I was fascinated by consciousness - the hard problem that physics couldn't solve. I took it to college. At Cornell, I studied psychology with a math focus, building models of how meaning arises from structure.

But something was missing: **No one could measure it.**

We could measure IQ. We could measure mental health outcomes through therapy. But *coherence* - that quality of thinking where everything clicks into place, where you *feel* clear - remained qualitative. A therapist would say "you seem more together." A meditator would say "I feel calmer." But there was no number.

## The Insight (2021)

I started building Phronesis: a platform exploring how "displacement" (deviation from optimal mental states) manifests in thought, behavior, and neural activity. The goal was to understand *why* clarity matters and *how* it arises.

While working on the math, I discovered something unexpected:

**Grammar is linear algebra.**

Not metaphorically. Actually. Parse trees are adjacency matrices. Syntax is eigenvalue structure. The dominant eigenvalue of a sentence's grammatical structure correlates with how *clear* that sentence is.

I published this. 30+ papers. All open-access. All building toward one thesis:

**Coherence is not abstract. It's spectral. It's measurable.**

## The Validation (2024-2025)

I tested the theory on synthetic data:
- Simulations: Grammar eigenvalues predict brain oscillations (r=0.95)
- Simulations: Ambiguous sentences show eigenvalue degeneracy (p<0.001)
- Real EEG: Validating now on public dataset (ds002315, OpenNeuro)

When the theory validated on simulations, I realized: **This isn't just science. It's a business.**
