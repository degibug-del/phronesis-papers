# Gumroad sales copy — the two-product structure

Built 2026-06-12. Files live in this folder: `essentials.pdf` (free), `phronesis-complete.pdf` (104pp, the paid book).
Recommendation: take down the nine separate paid courses; run exactly these two.

---

## PRODUCT 1 — free (the hook)

**Title:** Displacement Framework — Essentials

**Tagline:** One model for the gap between the life you meant to live and the one you're living — and the arithmetic that tells you when, and how, to close it.

**Price:** Free (PWYW, $0+).

**Description:**
You already know something is off — the plate, the feed, the inbox, the body, somewhere you're not where you meant to be. You don't need motivation. You need a way to think about it precise enough to act on.

This is the whole model in one short read: five parts, one theorem, three levers. By the end you can name any drift in your life in the same vocabulary, price it, and decide on paper — this week — whether to go back, when, and by which route.

It's free because it's the foundation. If it lands, the Complete book carries it into every domain.

---

## PRODUCT 2 — paid (the flagship)

**Title:** Phronesis — The Complete Course

**Tagline:** One model, carried from the theorem to the whole of a life: body, attention, energy, daily practice, love, money, ecology.

**Price:** PWYW, suggested $19 (your call — it's a 104-page book; $19–39 is fair).

**Description:**
104 pages. Ten chapters. One instrument.

Most advice is a pile of unrelated tips. This is the opposite: a single piece of machinery — ground state, displacement, the cost of return, the maintenance theorem — and then that one machine turned, chapter by chapter, on everything that actually drifts:

- **Maintenance Mastery** — the theorem itself, and the cadence that keeps anything from eroding
- **The Daily Return** — why the floor is one day, and how to land it
- **Attention** — the capture machine, and the return
- **Energy, Health, Love, Money, Ecology** — the same arithmetic, domain by domain
- **Applied** — putting it together

If Essentials gave you the vocabulary, this gives you the practice — priced once, kept for good. Buy it, keep it, re-read the chapter that matches whatever's drifting this season.

*Written fairly: no padding, no upsell, no manufactured urgency. One model, taught plainly, all the way down.*

---

### Why two and not ten
Ten SKUs split attention and cost you ten files to maintain. Two is legible: a free door, and one flagship that reads as a book worth keeping. Reduce, don't proliferate — one fair exchange.
