- Action: Download your institution's forms, paste content from `exp1_irb_protocol.md` into their template
- Fallback: Call your IRB office, ask "What information do you need from me?"

**"NSF grant is missing something"**
- Check: NSF Cognitive Neuroscience Program solicitation (search NSF.gov for "BCS-1416")
- Read: Their specific requirements section
- Add: Any missing sections (usually: conflict of interest forms, data management plan)

**"I can't find the EEG equipment"**
- Action: Check if your department has EEG capacity; if not, contact nearby university
- Fallback: Recruit a collaborator at another institution (get letter of support)
- Timeline: Must confirm by Aug 1 for Phase 1 to start Oct 1

**"IRB approval is taking too long"**
- Call: IRB office after 3 weeks (expected timeline 2–4 weeks)
- Ask: "Any issues with my protocol? Anything I can clarify?"
- Fallback: Delayed IRB approval = delayed recruitment, but science continues
